Support the Trust

Keeping one instrument in playing condition.

The Trust's work centres on maintaining a single instrument. Fundraising recitals go directly toward the cost of caring for the harpsichord.

The best way to support the Trust is by attending our fundraising events. See the Events page for details.

Trustees

Working to preserve the island's musical heritage.

The Trust is comprised of four Trustees: Susan Lloyd, Max Wong, Sally Ede-Golightly, and François Cloete.

Together the Trustees ensure the harpsichord's maintenance and organise events to promote the instrument. See the Events page for details.

Guernsey Harpsichord Trust — established 1977 to care for the island's Andrew Garlick harpsichord.
