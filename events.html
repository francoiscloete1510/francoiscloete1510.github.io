Concerts & recitals

The Trust's instrument is used for regular concerts on the island. Here's what's coming up.

2026 recital

French Connections, with François Cloete

Thursday 30 July 2026 · 6:30pm

French Connections

College Hall, Elizabeth College

Local organist François Cloete — currently organ scholar at Westminster Abbey — gives a harpsichord recital as a fundraiser for the Guernsey Harpsichord Trust. The programme explores harpsichord music in the French style by composers across Europe, including François Couperin, Henry Purcell, and J.S. Bach, and showcases the Trust's recently restored Andrew Garlick harpsichord.

François has waived his fee for the concert; donations of £25 or more are welcomed to help cover the cost of insuring and maintaining the instrument. Drinks will follow, with a chance to talk to François and inspect the instrument itself.

2025 recital

Bach's Goldberg Variations, with François Cloete

Saturday 2 August 2025 · 6:30pm

Goldberg Variations

College Hall, Elizabeth College

François Cloete - prizewinning graduate of the University of Oxford - gives a harpsichord recital of Bach's Goldberg Variations as a fundraiser for the Guernsey Harpsichord Trust. A work that contains 30 intricate variations upon an aria, it lasts an hour and a quarter in extremely rare performances where all variations are performed in a single sitting.

François joins an illustrious list of musicians who have performed this piece on piano but on the even more challenging medium of harpsichord, as originally intended by Bach.

Guernsey Harpsichord Trust — established 1977 to care for the island's Andrew Garlick harpsichord.
